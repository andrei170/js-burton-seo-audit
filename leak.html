Credit first: your form is better than most, with name, phone, email and text consent built in. The gap is everything that should happen next. Your site today

We looked at jsburton.org the way a prospective client would. Here is what is working, and the one layer it is missing.

What you've got

  • A genuinely strong content site (blog, FAQ, location pages)
  • A solid contact form with phone, email and text consent
  • Clear "free consultation" calls to action
  • Four office locations with directions

What's leaking

  • No online booking, an interested prospect has to wait for a callback
  • No lead magnet for the researcher who isn't ready to fill in a form yet
  • No automatic follow-up, so anyone who doesn't book on day one is gone
  • No instant response, so slower than the next firm they contact
Your form catches the ready-now buyer. In estate planning, most high-value clients are not ready-now; they read, they compare, they sit on it. Without a magnet to capture them and a sequence to follow up, those are the matters that quietly go to a competitor.

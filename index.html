The 5 fixes that close the gap

High impact

1Make your credentials machine-readable

Add Attorney and LegalService schema that explicitly states your awards (Top Lawyer Coastal Virginia 2024, National Trial Lawyers Top 10), 20+ years of experience, bar admissions and specialties. AI tools quote what they can parse. Right now these live in prose only.

High impact

2Add direct-answer content blocks

AI assistants lift clean, self-contained answers. Add concise "[Question], then a 40-word answer" blocks to your key pages (e.g. "Who is the best estate planning attorney in Virginia Beach?", "How much does a trust cost in Virginia?"). These are exactly the snippets that get cited.

Authority

3Strengthen third-party citations

AI engines triangulate trust from off-site mentions: Justia, Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers, Avvo, ACTEC, bar listings and local press. Where your competitors are densely listed, you are thin. We map the gaps and fill them.

Content

4Convert your blog into "answer" assets

You already publish. Reformatting your strongest posts into question-led, clearly-structured answers (with summaries, FAQs and lists) makes them far more likely to be quoted by AI Overviews and Perplexity, not just indexed.

Measurement

5Track AI citations, not just rankings

We set up monitoring across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity for your core terms, so you can see your firm move from "not mentioned" to "cited" over time, the same way you would track keyword rank.
